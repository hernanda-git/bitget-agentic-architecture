# Demo and signed-request probes

The files in this directory are quarantined and are not part of the normal executable
surface. They are retained for review history only. Do not run them as part of the
standalone paper or shadow workflows.

- They may access credentials or issue signed requests.
- They are not approved for demo or funded execution.
- Any future demo adapter must be typed, allow-listed, externally gated, and tested
  separately from offline launchers.
- No credentials are stored in this directory.
